LESSON 01 KATA - modes and survival
You are editing the COPY in /tmp/nvim-practice, not the file in the repo.
Lines in capitals are instructions. Change the lines underneath them.

EXERCISE 1 - i INSERTS BEFORE THE CURSOR, a APPENDS AFTER IT
Cursor on the s of jst: i u Esc. Then cursor on the l of lst: a i Esc.
jst --lst

EXERCISE 2 - I INSERTS AT THE START OF THE LINE, . REPEATS THE CHANGE
On rebuild: I, type "just " (with the space), Esc. Then j . and j . again.
rebuild
check
update

EXERCISE 3 - A APPENDS AT THE END OF THE LINE
Each session title needs a full stop: A . Esc, then j . and j . again.
Parse just's JSON dump with serde
Review the Codex source parser
Suggest clippy fixes for just-panel

EXERCISE 4 - o OPENS A LINE BELOW, O OPENS A LINE ABOVE
On the statix line: o, type "deadnix .", Esc. On lint-nix: O, type "# Lint Nix files", Esc.
lint-nix:
    statix check .

EXERCISE 5 - UNDO AND REDO
Press u until exercise 2 shows three bare recipe names again. Then Ctrl-r twice.

EXERCISE 6 - SAVE AND QUIT
Save with :w Enter, then change something and save with Ctrl-s (Normal mode only).
Quit with :q, reopen the file, and press u: the undo history survived.

EXERCISE 7 - A WINDOW IS NOT NEOVIM
:help i Enter opens a help window. Ctrl-q closes that window only; Neovim stays.
